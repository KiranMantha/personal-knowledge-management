You’ve seen the headlines: *“Coders are making $5K–$100K per month on Polymarket!”.* It sounds like printing money. I get the FOMO: if you can code, why not turn logic into cash?

***Spoiler: it’s not magic***. A tiny edge exists, but it’s very fragile, fast, and full of ways to lose. Here’s a systematic look at how these hacks supposedly work, why they’re tricky, and how to safely explore them yourself.

This is a continuation essay on [The Myth of Risk-free Polymarket and Kalshi Hacks As Money Machine](https://medium.com/@sipsnscale/the-myth-of-risk-free-polymarket-and-kalshi-hacks-as-money-machine-b487d86ea8ac). Enjoy!

## The Edge Hunting

The story is seductive: bots scan markets, find mispriced bets, and execute trades faster than humans. Profit stacks up. Some six-figure stories even pop up online.

I tried to take a deep breath and check the assumptions. That’s where the shine fades: the margin is tiny, and the moving parts are many.

Yes, programmers can extract small edges. No, consistent $100K months aren’t guaranteed. Only near-perfect execution makes it work.
