The web platform is far more powerful than most developers realize and every year it quietly gets new capabilities that change how we build things.

Funny enough, sometimes choosing what to write about is harder than writing the article itself.

This week I kept bouncing between two extremes:  
 either writing something that could be a total “banger”… or going deep into a heavy technical rabbit hole. 😅  
But I wanted something different.  
Still technical. Still practical.  
Just… lighter. Useful without requiring a 3-day research sprint.

And since I genuinely enjoy exploring what browsers can already do and how far we can push them without adding more dependencies I landed on a topic that feels both simple and surprisingly overlooked:

underused browser APIs.

Some of these might already be part of your daily workflow.  
But I’m pretty sure at least a few will make you pause and think,  
 “Wait… this exists natively?” 😉
